Privacy Policy

How Stacked collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated:

Apr 16, 2025

STACKED V1.0

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, and any details you submit through forms, waitlists, or account creation. We may also collect limited technical data automatically, including browser type, device information, and basic usage data, to help us understand how the platform is used and how it can be improved.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain the Stacked platform, communicate important updates or responses, improve functionality and performance, and protect the security and integrity of our services. Stacked does not sell personal data to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Stacked may use trusted third-party services to support features such as hosting, analytics, payments, or product integrations. These services may process limited data only as needed to perform their functions. We choose partners that are expected to handle data responsibly, but their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. While we work to safeguard your data, no online platform can guarantee complete security, and absolute protection cannot be ensured.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. These rights may include accessing the information we hold about you, requesting corrections, requesting deletion, or limiting how your data is used. You may also have the right to withdraw consent for certain types of data processing. Requests related to your data can be made by contacting us, and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to our practices, the platform, or applicable requirements. When updates occur, the revised version will be published on this page. Continued use of the website or services after changes are posted indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
